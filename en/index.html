Sky

A plain dusk becomes a violet afterglow with starbursts

The Sky tool detects the sky automatically and paints only the sky with a violet afterglow — the docks, boats and lights stay untouched. Then the Lens tool turns the lights into 8-point starbursts.

  • Sky: Sunset 60 (Precise mode)
  • Lens: Starburst 100, 8 points
  • Basic: Vibrance +25, Contrast +10
BeforeAfter
Fireworks

Clear the smoke from your fireworks

The longer the show runs, the more smoke hides the bursts. Remove Smoke subtracts the large, smooth smoke layer while keeping every fine spark — and only touches the sky, so buildings and lights stay as they are.

  • Shadow Detail: Remove Smoke 100
BeforeAfter
Stack + Smoke

Four bursts in one frame — smoke gone

Four fireworks shots from the same tripod spot are merged in Stack's Fireworks mode — which stacks the smoke too. Add Remove Smoke and the sky turns clean black, with every burst blooming at once.

  • Stack: Fireworks mode (4 frames)
  • Shadow Detail: Remove Smoke 100

FAQ

Are my photos uploaded?

No. Everything is processed on your device; the app has no upload feature.

Does it change the originals in my library?

No. Editing is non-destructive; exporting saves a new photo.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone and iPad. AI features run faster on newer models.

Which formats are supported?

Import photos from your library and camera RAW files; export to JPEG, HEIC, PNG or TIFF, optionally keeping EXIF and the color profile.

Is PRO a subscription?

No. Buy PRO once and keep it forever.
